• Here is how it should be prototyped:
char *ft_strncpy(char *dest, char *src, unsigned int n);

Your function must be declared as follows:
    char *ft_strncpy(char *dest, char *src, unsigned int n);

Examples:
    char d[8]="xxxxxxx"; ft_strncpy(d,"abc",5); printf("%c%c%c:%d:%d\n",d[0],d[1],d[2],d[3],d[4])

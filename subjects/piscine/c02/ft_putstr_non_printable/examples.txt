# Examples for ft_putstr_non_printable (from official subject specification)

example:
Hello\nHow are you?
• The function should display:
Hello\0aHow are you?
• Here is how it should be prototyped:
void ft_putstr_non_printable(char *str);

Your function must be declared as follows:
    void ft_putstr_non_printable(char *str);

Examples:
    ft_putstr_non_printable("Coucou\ntu vas bien ?")
    Output: Coucou\0atu vas bien ?
    ft_putstr_non_printable("A\tB\nC\177D")
    Output: A\09B\0aC\7fD
    ft_putstr_non_printable("Printable 42!")
    Output: Printable 42!
    ft_putstr_non_printable(""); write(1, "|", 1); ft_putstr_non_printable("a\nb"); write(1, "|", 1); ft_putstr_non_printable(" \t "); write(1, "\n", 1)
    Output: |a\0ab| \09

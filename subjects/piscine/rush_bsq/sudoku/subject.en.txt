Assignment name  : sudoku
Expected files   : *.c *.h
Allowed functions: write, malloc, free
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that resolves a 9x9 Sudoku puzzle passed on the command line.

Your program takes 9 arguments, each representing one row of the grid as a
9-character string containing only digits from '1' to '9' and the '.' character
for empty cells.

If the input grid is valid and solvable, your program must display the solved
9x9 grid as 9 lines of 9 digits separated by a single space, each ending with
a newline.

If the number of arguments is different from 9, if any argument does not have
exact length 9, if an invalid character is encountered, if initial clues contain
duplicates in a row, column, or 3x3 subgrid, or if the puzzle has no valid
solution, the program must display "Error" followed by a newline.

Compilation:
    - Your program will be compiled with: cc -Wall -Wextra -Werror *.c

Examples:
$> ./sudoku "9...7...." "2...9..53" ".6..124.." "84...1.9." "5.....8.." ".31..4..." "..37..68." ".9..5.741" "47......." | cat -e
9 1 4 3 7 5 2 6 8$
2 8 7 4 9 6 1 5 3$
3 6 5 8 1 2 4 7 9$
8 4 6 5 2 1 3 9 7$
5 2 9 6 3 7 8 1 4$
7 3 1 9 8 4 5 2 6$
1 5 3 7 4 9 6 8 2$
6 9 8 2 5 3 7 4 1$
4 7 2 1 6 8 9 3 5$
$> ./sudoku "9...7...." | cat -e
Error$

• Place boxes of heights 1 to 4 on a 4x4 grid so that each row and column contains
  each height exactly once, satisfying all 16 visibility clues around the perimeter.
• Argument format (single string of 16 space-separated integers between 1 and 4):
  "col1up col2up col3up col4up col1down col2down col3down col4down row1left row2left row3left row4left row1right row2right row3right row4right"
• In case of any error (invalid arguments or unsolvable grid), display "Error\n".

Example:
$> ./rush-01 "4 3 2 1 1 2 2 2 4 3 2 1 1 2 2 2" | cat -e
1 2 3 4$
2 3 4 1$
3 4 1 2$
4 1 2 3$

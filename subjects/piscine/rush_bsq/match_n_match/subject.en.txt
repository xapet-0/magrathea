# match_n_match

Build a `match-n-match` binary. It takes two arguments, a string and a pattern, then prints `match: <0|1>` for exact matching and `nmatch: <0|1>` for wildcard matching where `*` matches any sequence and `?` matches one character.

The project must include a Makefile. Source files may be at the project root or under `srcs/` and headers may be at the project root or under `includes/`.
Generated objects, binaries other than the expected binary, correction folders, and test folders must not be submitted.

Your function must be declared as follows:
    int match(char *s1, char *s2); int nmatch(char *s1, char *s2);

Examples:
    printf("%d:%d:%d:%d\n", match("main.c", "*.c"), match("main.c", "*.h"), nmatch("abcbd", "*b*"), nmatch("abc", "a**"))
    Output: 1:0:2:3

Assignment name  : count_digits_sh
Expected files   : count_digits_sh.sh
Allowed commands : printf, tr, wc
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that counts and prints the number of decimal digits (0-9)
contained in the environment variable FT_LINE, followed by a newline, without
spaces.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> FT_LINE="1337 Benguerir 42" sh count_digits_sh.sh | cat -e
6$
$> FT_LINE="no digits" sh count_digits_sh.sh | cat -e
0$
$> FT_LINE="0123456789" sh count_digits_sh.sh | cat -e
10$
$> FT_LINE="" sh count_digits_sh.sh | cat -e
0$

Assignment name  : alien_math
Expected files   : alien_math.sh
Allowed commands : echo, printf, tr, bc, expr
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that adds the integer values stored in the environment
variables FT_NBR1 and FT_NBR2 and prints their sum followed by a newline.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> FT_NBR1="1" FT_NBR2="1" sh alien_math.sh | cat -e
2$
$> FT_NBR1="40" FT_NBR2="2" sh alien_math.sh | cat -e
42$
$> FT_NBR1="1300" FT_NBR2="37" sh alien_math.sh | cat -e
1337$

Assignment name  : mirror_sort
Expected files   : mirror_sort.sh
Allowed commands : rev, sort
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that reads lines from standard input, reverses each line,
and sorts the reversed lines in alphabetical order.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> printf "ba\nab\n" | sh mirror_sort.sh | cat -e
ab$
ba$
$> printf "dc\nba\n" | sh mirror_sort.sh | cat -e
ab$
cd$
$> printf "z\na\n" | sh mirror_sort.sh | cat -e
a$
z$

Assignment name  : wheeler_loop
Expected files   : wheeler_loop.sh
Allowed commands : printf
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a POSIX shell script that safely iterates over all .txt files in the
current directory (for f in ./*.txt) and prints each filename stripped of its
leading ./ prefix, even when filenames contain spaces.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> sh wheeler_loop.sh | cat -e
./a.txt$

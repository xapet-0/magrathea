Assignment name  : env_expander
Expected files   : env_expander.sh
Allowed commands : env, sed, awk, envsubst, sh
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that reads lines from standard input and expands $FT_VAR
with the value of the environment variable FT_VAR.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> printf "Hello $FT_VAR\n" | FT_VAR="1337" sh env_expander.sh | cat -e
Hello 1337$
$> printf "Welcome to $FT_VAR!\n" | FT_VAR="42_School" sh env_expander.sh | cat -e
Welcome to 42_School!$
$> printf "$FT_VAR\n" | FT_VAR="OK" sh env_expander.sh | cat -e
OK$

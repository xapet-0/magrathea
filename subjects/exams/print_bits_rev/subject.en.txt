Assignment name  : print_bits_rev
Expected files   : print_bits_rev.c
Allowed functions: write
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a function that takes a byte (unsigned char octet) and prints its 8
bits in reverse order (from least significant bit to most significant bit) on
standard output, without a newline at the end.

Your function must be declared as follows:
    void print_bits_rev(unsigned char octet);

Examples:
    print_bits_rev(2); printf("\n")
    Output: 01000000
    print_bits_rev(0); printf("\n")
    Output: 00000000
    print_bits_rev(255); printf("\n")
    Output: 11111111
    print_bits_rev(1); printf("\n")
    Output: 10000000

Assignment name  : naahio
Expected files   : naahio.c
Allowed functions: write
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that receives one string as an argument (which may use underscores '_' as word separators).

Your program:
    - Replaces '_' with spaces ' ',
    - Reverses the order of the words,
    - Counts the vowels,
    - Prints the final message and the vowel count.

Examples:
$> ./naahio Hello_Open_AI_is_cool | cat -e
Reversed: cool is AI Open Hello$
Vowel count: 9$

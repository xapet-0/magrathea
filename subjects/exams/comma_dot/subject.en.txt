Assignment name  : comma_dot
Expected files   : comma_dot.sh
Allowed commands : tr, sed, paste
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Write a shell script that reads lines from standard input and joins them into a
single line separated by ", " (a comma and a space), ending with a "." (dot)
and NO trailing newline.

Examples:
$> printf "alpha\nbeta\ngamma\n" | ./comma_dot.sh | cat -e
alpha, beta, gamma.$>

Assignment name  : mini_shell_sh
Expected files   : mini_shell_sh.sh
Allowed commands : sh, read, eval, printf, echo
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a minimal POSIX shell loop script that reads commands line by line from
standard input and executes them until exit or EOF is reached.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> printf "echo 42\nexit\n" | sh mini_shell_sh.sh | cat -e
42$
$> printf "echo hello\necho 1337\nexit\n" | sh mini_shell_sh.sh | cat -e
hello$
1337$
$> printf "exit\n" | sh mini_shell_sh.sh | cat -e
$

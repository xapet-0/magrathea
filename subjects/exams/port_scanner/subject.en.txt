Assignment name  : port_scanner
Expected files   : port_scanner.sh
Allowed commands : ss, netstat, awk, sed, sort, printf
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that scans or reports listening local TCP ports (printing
OPEN when verified).

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> sh port_scanner.sh "80" | cat -e
80$

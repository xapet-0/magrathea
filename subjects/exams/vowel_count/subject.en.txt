Assignment name  : vowel_count
Expected files   : vowel_count.sh
Allowed commands : printf, tr, wc
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that counts and prints the number of vowels (a, e, i, o, u
in both lowercase and uppercase) contained in the environment variable FT_LINE,
followed by a newline and without spaces.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> FT_LINE="Hello 1337 World!" sh vowel_count.sh | cat -e
3$
$> FT_LINE="AEIOUaeiou" sh vowel_count.sh | cat -e
10$
$> FT_LINE="bcdfg" sh vowel_count.sh | cat -e
0$
$> FT_LINE="" sh vowel_count.sh | cat -e
0$

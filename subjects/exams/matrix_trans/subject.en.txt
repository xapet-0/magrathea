Assignment name  : matrix_trans
Expected files   : matrix_trans.sh
Allowed commands : awk
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that reads a space-separated matrix from standard input
and prints its transpose (converting rows into columns and columns into rows).

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> printf "1 2\n3 4\n" | sh matrix_trans.sh | cat -e
1 3$
2 4$
$> printf "1 2 3\n4 5 6\n" | sh matrix_trans.sh | cat -e
1 4$
2 5$
3 6$
$> printf "a b\nc d\ne f\n" | sh matrix_trans.sh | cat -e
a c e$
b d f$

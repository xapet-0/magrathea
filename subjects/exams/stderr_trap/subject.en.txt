Assignment name  : stderr_trap
Expected files   : stderr_trap.sh
Allowed commands : sh, printf, echo
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that redirects error messages appropriately and prints the
expected diagnostic output.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> sh stderr_trap.sh | cat -e
captured$

Assignment name  : skip_even
Expected files   : skip_even.sh
Allowed commands : awk, sed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that reads from standard input and displays only
odd-numbered lines (1, 3, 5...), skipping every even-numbered line.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> printf "line1\nline2\nline3\n" | sh skip_even.sh | cat -e
line1$
line3$
$> printf "a\nb\nc\nd\ne\n" | sh skip_even.sh | cat -e
a$
c$
e$
$> printf "only1\n" | sh skip_even.sh | cat -e
only1$

Assignment name  : env_compass
Expected files   : env_compass.sh
Allowed commands : printf, uname, id, pwd
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that displays the environment identity formatted as
$USER:$HOME:$PWD followed by a newline.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> USER="marvin" HOME="/home/marvin" PWD="/tmp" sh env_compass.sh | cat -e
marvin:/home/marvin:/tmp$
$> USER="student" HOME="/Users/student" PWD="/var" sh env_compass.sh | cat -e
student:/Users/student:/var$
$> USER="root" HOME="/root" PWD="/" sh env_compass.sh | cat -e
root:/root:/$

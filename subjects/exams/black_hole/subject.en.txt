Assignment name  : black_hole
Expected files   : black_hole.sh
Allowed commands : printf, echo, ls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that redirects error output to /dev/null (the system
black hole) and prints OK followed by a newline on standard output.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> sh black_hole.sh | cat -e
OK$
$> sh black_hole.sh "ignored_err" | cat -e
OK$
$> sh black_hole.sh "a" "b" | cat -e
OK$

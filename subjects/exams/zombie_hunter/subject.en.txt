Assignment name  : zombie_hunter
Expected files   : zombie_hunter.sh
Allowed commands : ps, awk, printf
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that inspects the process table for zombie processes (Z
state) or outputs 0 when no zombie processes are present.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> printf "Z 1042\nS 1001\n" | sh zombie_hunter.sh | cat -e
1042$

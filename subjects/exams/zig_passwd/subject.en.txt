Assignment name  : zig_passwd
Expected files   : zig_passwd.sh
Allowed commands : grep, awk, cut, rev, sort, paste, sed, tr
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that reads passwd-formatted lines from standard input,
ignores comment lines starting with '#', extracts the login field (field 1
separated by ':'), and prints the logins.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> printf "root:x:0:0::/root:/bin/sh\n" | sh zig_passwd.sh | cat -e
root$
$> printf "marvin:x:42:42::/home/marvin:/bin/sh\nbocal:x:1337:1337::/home/bocal:/bin/sh\n" | sh zig_passwd.sh | cat -e
marvin$
bocal$

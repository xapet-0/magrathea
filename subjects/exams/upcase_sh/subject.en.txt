Assignment name  : upcase_sh
Expected files   : upcase_sh.sh
Allowed commands : printf, tr
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that converts all lowercase letters in the environment
variable FT_LINE to uppercase and prints the result followed by a newline.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> FT_LINE="hello 42" sh upcase_sh.sh | cat -e
HELLO 42$
$> FT_LINE="AbCdEf" sh upcase_sh.sh | cat -e
ABCDEF$
$> FT_LINE="1337!" sh upcase_sh.sh | cat -e
1337!$

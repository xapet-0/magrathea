Assignment name  : find_clean
Expected files   : find_clean.sh
Allowed commands : find, rm
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that searches the current directory and all its
subdirectories for backup/temporary files ending with ~ or starting and
ending with #, and prints their relative paths.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> sh find_clean.sh | cat -e
./junk~$
./#tmp#$
$> sh find_clean.sh | cat -e
./a~$

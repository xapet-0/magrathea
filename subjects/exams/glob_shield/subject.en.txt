Assignment name  : glob_shield
Expected files   : glob_shield.sh
Allowed commands : printf
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a POSIX shell script that prints the exact first argument `$1` (or
`$FT_PAYLOAD`) followed by a newline, preserving all multiple consecutive
spaces and literal `*` or `?` characters without pathname expansion.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> sh glob_shield.sh "a * b   c" | cat -e
a * b   c$
$> sh glob_shield.sh "Hello    *    World?" | cat -e
Hello    *    World?$
$> sh glob_shield.sh "1337   42" | cat -e
1337   42$

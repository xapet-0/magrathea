# Examples for size_variables (from official subject specification)

Example:

$> ./size_variable 'int' | cat -e
4 bytes$
$> ./size_variable 'char' | cat -e
1 bytes$
$> ./size_variable 'float' | cat -e
4 bytes$
$>./size_variable 'double'| cat -e
8 bytes$
$>./size_variable 'tryu'| cat -e
$
$>./size_variable "" | cat -e
$

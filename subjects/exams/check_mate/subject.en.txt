Assignment name  : check_mate
Expected files   : *.c *.h
Allowed functions: write, malloc, free
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that takes rows of a chessboard as arguments and checks if your
King is in a check position.

Chess is played on a squared board with specific pieces on it: King, Queen,
Bishop, Rook and Pawns. For this exercise, you will only play with Pawns,
Bishops, Rooks, Queens, and a King.

Each piece has a specific method of movement, and all patterns of capture are
detailed in the examples below and in examples.txt.

A piece can capture only the first enemy piece it finds on its capture
patterns.

The board has a variable size but will remain a square. There is only one King
('K') and all other pieces are against it. All other characters except those
used for pieces ('P', 'B', 'R', 'Q', 'K') are considered empty squares.

The King is considered in check when an enemy piece can capture it. In that
case, print "Success" on standard output followed by a newline; otherwise print
"Fail" followed by a newline.

If there are no arguments, the program displays only a newline.

Examples:
$> ./check_mate '..' '.K' | cat -e
Fail$
$> ./check_mate 'R...' '..P.' '.K..' '....' | cat -e
Success$
$> ./check_mate 'R...' 'iheK' '....' 'jeiR' | cat -e
Success$
$> ./check_mate | cat -e
$

Capture patterns:
  Pawn (P):
	. . . . . . .
	. . . . . . .
	. . X . X . .
	. . . P . . .
	. . . . . . .
	. . . . . . .
	. . . . . . .

  Bishop (B):
	X . . . . . X
	. X . . . X .
	. . X . X . .
	. . . B . . .
	. . X . X . .
	. X . . . X .
	X . . . . . X

  Rook (R):
	. . . X . . .
	. . . X . . .
	. . . X . . .
	X X X R X X X
	. . . X . . .
	. . . X . . .
	. . . X . . .

  Queen (Q):
	X . . X . . X
	. X . X . X .
	. . X X X . .
	X X X Q X X X
	. . X X X . .
	. X . X . X .
	X . . X . . X

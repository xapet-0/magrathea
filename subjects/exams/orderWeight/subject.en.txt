Assignment name  : orderWeight
Expected files   : orderWeight.c
Allowed functions: malloc, free
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Given a string of space-separated positive integers, return a newly allocated
string with the numbers sorted by the sum of their digits ("weight") in
ascending order. When two numbers have the same digit sum, sort them
lexicographically as strings.

Your function must be declared as follows:

char	*orderWeight(const char *strng);

Example:
  orderWeight("56 65 74 100 99 68 86 180 90")
  -> "100 180 90 56 65 74 68 86 99"

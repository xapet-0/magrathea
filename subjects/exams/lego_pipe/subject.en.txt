Assignment name  : lego_pipe
Expected files   : lego_pipe.sh
Allowed commands : printf, tr, sort, uniq
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that reads lines from standard input, removes empty lines,
converts uppercase letters to lowercase, and outputs the unique lines in
alphabetical order.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> printf "B\na\nB\n\n" | sh lego_pipe.sh | cat -e
a$
b$
$> printf "Z\nA\nz\nA\n" | sh lego_pipe.sh | cat -e
a$
z$
$> printf "hello\nHELLO\nworld\n" | sh lego_pipe.sh | cat -e
hello$
world$

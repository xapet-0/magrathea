Assignment name  : epur_str_sh
Expected files   : epur_str_sh.sh
Allowed commands : echo, printf, awk, sed, tr
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that takes the string in FT_LINE, removes leading and
trailing whitespace, collapses multiple spaces into a single space, and prints
the result followed by a newline.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> FT_LINE="   hello    world   " sh epur_str_sh.sh | cat -e
hello world$
$> FT_LINE="1337   42   piscine" sh epur_str_sh.sh | cat -e
1337 42 piscine$
$> FT_LINE="single" sh epur_str_sh.sh | cat -e
single$

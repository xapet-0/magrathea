Assignment name  : occ_a_sh
Expected files   : occ_a_sh.sh
Allowed commands : printf, tr, wc
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that counts and prints the number of occurrences of the
lowercase character 'a' in the environment variable FT_LINE, followed by a
newline, without spaces.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> FT_LINE="abracadabra" sh occ_a_sh.sh | cat -e
5$
$> FT_LINE="xyz" sh occ_a_sh.sh | cat -e
0$
$> FT_LINE="aaa" sh occ_a_sh.sh | cat -e
3$
$> FT_LINE="" sh occ_a_sh.sh | cat -e
0$

Assignment name  : freq_rank
Expected files   : freq_rank.sh
Allowed commands : tr, sort, uniq, awk, sed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that reads words/lines from standard input, counts the
occurrences of each word, and prints them sorted by frequency in descending
order as <count> <word>.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> printf "b\na\nb\n" | sh freq_rank.sh | cat -e
2 b$
1 a$
$> printf "x\nx\nx\ny\n" | sh freq_rank.sh | cat -e
3 x$
1 y$
$> printf "42\n" | sh freq_rank.sh | cat -e
1 42$

Assignment name  : odd_epur
Expected files   : odd_epur.sh
Allowed commands : awk, sed, tr
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that reads from standard input, keeps only odd-numbered
lines (1, 3, 5...), trims leading and trailing spaces, and collapses multiple
spaces between words into a single space.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> printf "  hello   world  \nignore\n  foo   bar  \n" | sh odd_epur.sh | cat -e
hello world$
foo bar$
$> printf "   42   1337   \n" | sh odd_epur.sh | cat -e
42 1337$
$> printf "a   b\nc   d\ne   f\n" | sh odd_epur.sh | cat -e
a b$
e f$

# Examples for count_of_2 (from official subject specification)

example, for n = 25, the digit
'2' appears in 2, 12, 20, 21, 22 (twice), 23, 24, 25 -> 9 times.

Your function must be declared as follows:
    int count_of_2(int n);

Examples:
    count_of_2(25) -> 9
    count_of_2(1) -> 0
    count_of_2(2) -> 1
    count_of_2(12) -> 2

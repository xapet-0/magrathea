Assignment name  : word_count_sh
Expected files   : word_count_sh.sh
Allowed commands : printf, echo, wc, tr
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that displays the number of words contained in the
environment variable FT_LINE, followed by a newline, without spaces.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> FT_LINE="Hello   world 1337" sh word_count_sh.sh | cat -e
3$
$> FT_LINE="   " sh word_count_sh.sh | cat -e
0$
$> FT_LINE="one two three four" sh word_count_sh.sh | cat -e
4$
$> FT_LINE="single" sh word_count_sh.sh | cat -e
1$

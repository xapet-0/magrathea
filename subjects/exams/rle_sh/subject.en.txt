Assignment name  : rle_sh
Expected files   : rle_sh.sh
Allowed commands : printf, awk, sed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a shell script that reads the string in $FT_LINE and compresses
consecutive identical characters using Run-Length Encoding (<count><char>),
followed by a newline.

Requirements:
    - Your solution must be POSIX-compliant (/bin/sh) and handle spaces, tabs, and special characters safely.
    - Do not leave temporary files behind in the working directory.

Examples:
$> FT_LINE="aaabbc" sh rle_sh.sh | cat -e
3a2b1c$
$> FT_LINE="xxxx" sh rle_sh.sh | cat -e
4x$
$> FT_LINE="ab" sh rle_sh.sh | cat -e
1a1b$
